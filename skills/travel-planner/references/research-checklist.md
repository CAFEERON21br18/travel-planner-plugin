# Research checklist

Open every page you take a fact from. Record `source` (URL) and `checked` (today) on stops and budget lines.

| Topic | Where to look | Lands in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry rules and documents (ID, visa, EES/ETIAS, vaccines) for the travellers' nationality | Official government / EU pages | to-dos (category `documents`) with links |
| Getting there and between bases | Web search for routes, operators, typical fares and durations | choices round; budget `getting-there` (`indicative`); to-do "Book …" with a pre-filled search link |
| Where to sleep | Official sites and booking pages, 2–3 per base | choices round; `lodging`; budget `sleep`; to-do "Book … by …" |
| Sights | Official sites: hours, closed days, price, timed tickets, time needed | `days[].stops` (`hours`, `closed`, `price`, `link`); timed tickets → to-do with a "book by" date |
| Food | 1–2 options near each lunch and dinner slot | stops of kind `food` |
| Holidays and events on the dates | `https://date.nager.at/api/v3/PublicHolidays/<year>/<CC>` + web search | closures; `days[].tips` |
| Climate for the month | Web search for monthly averages | `packing` |
| Driving | Tolls, vignettes, low-emission zones, parking; fuel price per litre in the country | `practical.notes`; budget `car` (km from the build × consumption of the car model × fuel price) |
| Rental car | The firm's age and licence conditions, deposit | to-do (category `documents` or `book`) |
| Practical | Emergency number, currency, plugs, roaming, useful apps | `practical` |

Never: invent a price, an opening hour or a place; use Google Places results as data (they may only be shown in Google's own card); put friends' contacts into any file.
